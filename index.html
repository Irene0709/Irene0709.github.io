About

Why is some winter weather
predictable?

My PhD showed how stratosphere–troposphere coupling and other remote drivers shape the subseasonal predictability of Northern Eurasian cold spells. I used reforecasts, relaxation (nudging) experiments with ECMWF's OpenIFS, and process-based verification. Now I extend this work to applications such as district heating demand, and to machine-learning forecast systems.

Subseasonal-to-seasonal prediction Stratosphere–troposphere coupling Cold extremes Forecast verification Machine learning for weather
